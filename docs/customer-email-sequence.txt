HOW THE SEQUENCE WORKS

Immediately after an order is placed, the customer receives one confirmation:

  A. Paid online orders receive "Your Yes Bakery order is confirmed."
  B. Pay-at-pickup orders receive "Your Yes Bakery pickup order [ORDER ID]."

The remaining emails are shared by both order types. They are sent when the
order is moved through the corresponding status in the bakery backend:

  2. In Progress  -> "Your Yes Bakery order is being processed"
  3. Done         -> "Your Yes Bakery order is ready for pickup"
  4. Picked Up    -> "Thank you for picking up your Yes Bakery order"

The bracketed text below represents customer- or order-specific information.
